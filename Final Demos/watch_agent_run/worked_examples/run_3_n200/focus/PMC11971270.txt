L66|BMDMs are assumed to be nearly 100% pure as they are bone marrow cells differentiated into macrophages over seven days with M-CSF.
L66|Bone marrow cells were differentiated into BMDMs on petri dishes using 40 ng/mL recombinant M-CSF (BioLegend, Cat# 576406) and 1 µg/ml 4-Hydroxytamoxifen (Millipore Sigma, Cat# SML1666) in the same media formulation as TR-AMs.
