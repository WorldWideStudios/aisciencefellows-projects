L74:Culture of bone marrow derived macrophages (BMDM)
L76:This step includes washing out and dissociating the bone marrow resulting in a homogenous cell suspension, which can then be plated in culture flasks. Note:  Dissection of one mouse is required to obtain a confluent and homogeneous culture in a petri dish. These cells can be used as a reference to the isolated tissue-resident macrophages in several assays. 137. With sharp scissors carefully cut off the hind legs from the hip joint without damaging the femur. Place the legs in a 50 mL falcon filled with PBS and leave on ice until processing. 138. Take the legs out of PBS and discard any excess skin and muscle. 139. Femur and tibia were separated from each other and the fibula and tendons were removed. 140. To reduce the risk of contamination, shortly dip the bones in 70% ethanol followed by fresh sterile PBS. 141. Cut off a small piece of the bone on both sides (around 1 mm) using a bone scissor to open up the cavity inside the bone containing the bone marrow. 142. Flush out the bone marrow by picking up the bone with forceps and introducing a 0,45 mm thick needle connected to a 20 mL disposable syringe filled with ice cold PBS through a 100 μm filter into a 50 mL falcon. 143. Centrifuge the cells for 5 min with 300 g at 4°C. 144. Discard the supernatant and the cell pellet and resuspend it in 1 mL of red blood cell (RBC) lysis buffer. 145. Incubate the cell suspension on ice for 5 min. 146. Add 9 mL fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) buffer to the suspension and mix it by inversion. 147. Centrifuge the cells at 300 g for 5 min at 4°C. 148. Discard the supernatant. 149. Resuspend the cells in 10 mL BMDM medium. 150. Culture cells from one leg in one 10 cm cell culture-treated Petri dish. 151. Let the dish sit under the cell culture hood at RT for 30 min. 152. Put the dish in the normoxic incubator at 37°C. 153. After two days, change the medium and put the dish back into the incubator. 154. After seven days, BMDM are ready for assays.
