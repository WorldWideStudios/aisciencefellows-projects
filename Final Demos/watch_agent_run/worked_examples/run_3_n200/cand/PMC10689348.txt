L36|Generation of bone marrow-derived macrophages
L37|Bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) were prepared as described previously ( 17 ).
L37|In brief, bone marrow cells were aseptically isolated from mouse femora and cultured (5 × 10 5  cells per ml) in Dulbecco’s modified Eagle’s medium (DMEM) supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum and 2 ng ml −1  macrophage colony-stimulating factor (Peprotech, Rocky Hill, NJ, USA) in a temperature-responsive 60-mm culture dish (RepCell, CellSeed, Tokyo, Japan); cells were incubated at 37°C in humidified 5% CO 2  for 6–10 days for differentiation into macrophages.
