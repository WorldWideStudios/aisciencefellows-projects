L1|Diabetic wound healing via a co-delivery system for bone marrow derived stem cells and euphorbia hirta extract: an in vitro and in vivo study
L7|In the current study, a nanofibrous scaffold was developed for the co-delivery of bone marrow-derived stem cells and Euphorbia hirta extract into the diabetic wound beds.
L7|A wound healing study showed that scaffolds loaded with both bone marrow-derived stem cells and euphorbia hirta extract had the highest wound healing activity, as shown by significantly higher wound closure percentage, collagen deposition, and epithelial thickness.
