L26:Recently, Fleetwood et al. compared the different responses of murine bone marrow-derived macrophages subsets GM-BMϕ and M-BMϕ to LPS stimulation. After LPS stimulation, GM-BMϕ preferentially produced TNF-α, IL-6, IL-12p70, and IL-23 whereas, while M-BMϕ generated more IL-10 and CCL2 under similar conditions. Interestingly, phenotypes of GM-BMϕ and M-BMϕ adopt the phenotype of other populations if pre-treated with M-CSF and GM-CSF, respectively. This indicates the plasticity of GM-BMϕ and M-BMϕ by M-CSF and GM-CSF, and further supports the argument that GM-BMϕ may be the dominant macrophage subset during the inflammatory reaction [ 23 ].
