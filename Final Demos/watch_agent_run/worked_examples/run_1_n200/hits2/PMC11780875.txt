L136:Induction and Identification of Adipogenic, Osteogenic, and Chondrogenic Differentiation of Bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells (BMMSCs)
L137:BMMSC differentiation experiments were conducted using commercial kits. Specifically, the OriCell® Adipogenic Differentiation Kit (Cyagen, MUXMX-90031) was used to induce adipogenic differentiation, the OriCell® Osteogenic Differentiation Kit (Cyagen, MUXMX-90021) was used for osteogenic differentiation, and the OriCell® Chondrogenic Differentiation Kit (Cyagen, MUXMX-90041) was used for chondrogenic differentiation. Following induction, Oil Red O staining, alizarin red staining, and alcian blue staining were used to identify BMMSC adipogenesis, osteogenesis, and chondrogenesis, respectively.
L139:BMMSC surface molecules were detected using the OriCell® BMSC (Mouse) Surface Marker Detection Kit (Cyagen, MUXMX-09011). Specifically, cell suspensions were incubated with primary antibodies targeting positive cell surface markers, including CD44, CD29, and Sca-1, as well as negative marker primary antibodies targeting CD31, CD117, and CD34. Isotype control antibodies were also included in the incubation process. The incubation was conducted at 4°C for 30 min. Cells were subsequently incubated with FITC/PE-labeled secondary antibodies for 30 min at 4°C in the dark. Flow cytometry was used to detect the expression of cell surface molecules.
L141:All cells were gradually passaged and expanded to T225 culture flasks (Corning, 431082). When approximately 50% confluence was reached, the original culture medium was discarded, and the cells were rinsed three times with 5 mL of PBS (HyClone, SH30256.01). Subsequently, the medium was changed to a DMEM/F-12 medium (Gibco, 11320033), which contained 10% exosome-free fetal bovine serum (Cyagen, MUXMX-05002). The cells were then cultured until they reached approximately 80% confluence. Cell culture supernatants were collected and subjected to several sequential centrifugation steps to isolate extracellular vesicles (EVs). First, the supernatant was centrifuged at 300 × g, 4°C, for 10 min to remove cells, followed by centrifugation at 3000 × g, 4°C for 10 min to remove cellular debris. The resulting supernatant was then filtered through a 0.22 μm filter, and EVs were concentrated using a 100 kD ultrafiltration tube. Concentrated EVs were pelleted by ultracentrifugation (BECKMAN COULTER, OptimaTM MAX-XP Ultracentrifuge) at 100,000 × g, 4°C, for 2 h. L160:A 24-mm Transwell® cell culture plate (Corning, 3412) with a membrane pore size of 0.4 μm was used for the experiment. BMSCs were seeded in the upper chamber, and RAW264.7 cells were seeded in the lower chamber at an initial cell seeding ratio of 2:1. To induce the RAW264.7 foam cell model, a complete medium containing high glucose (33.3 mmol/L) and oxidized low-density lipoprotein (ox-LDL, 75 μg/mL) (Yiyuan, YB-002) was added to the lower chamber. The EV secretion inhibitor GW4869 (MCE, HY-19363) was used to analyze the role of EVs on the effects of BMMSCs on RAW264.7 cells. For Oil Red O stains of RAW264.7 cells co-cultured with BMMSCs, cell slides were placed in the lower chamber before they were seeded.
