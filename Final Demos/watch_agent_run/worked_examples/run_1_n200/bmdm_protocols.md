# Mouse BMDM differentiation protocols extracted from 200 PMC papers

Scope: in-vitro differentiation of **mouse** bone marrow into macrophages (BMDM/BMM).
Excluded (listed at the end): BMDC protocols, osteoclast-only cultures, non-mouse species,
ES/iPS-derived macrophages, and papers that use BMDMs without describing how they were made.
