L29:Human Monocyte-Derived Macrophages (hMDMs) are obtained with a density gradient centrifugation using a Ficoll-gradient method. This method relies on the different densities of blood components for their separation in distinct phases. The Ficoll solution allows the partition of erythrocytes and polynuclear cells at the lower part of the tube due to their density being higher than Ficoll while plasma and peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) lay on top of it. Monocytes (CD14+ cells) can then be isolated from PBMCs by positive magnetic sorting. Subsequently they can be differentiated into human primary macrophages, hMDMs, by culturing them in the presence of recombinant human M-CSF (rh-MCSF) ( Figure 1 ). 1. Cell isolation: Separation of PBMCs from whole blood (or buffy coat). a. Dilute blood with equal volume of PBS (at room temperature, RT). b. Add 15 mL of Lymphocytes Separation Medium in 50 mL Falcon tubes (RT). c. Pipette slowly 30 mL of PBS-diluted blood over the separation medium, be careful not to mix both phases. Note:  For volumes less than 30 mL of PBS-diluted blood, use a Ficoll to blood ratio of 1:2 and adjust all volumes accordingly. d.
