L22:Bone-marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) were generated from 8- to 12-week-old BALB/c male wild-type mice (CLEA Japan Inc., Tokyo, Japan) and Tlr2 knockout mice (Oriental Bioservice, Inc., Kyoto, Japan) as described previously, with some modifications ( 17 ). In brief, bone marrow cells were harvested from femurs. Cells were cultured in 90 × 15-mm vented petri dishes (Sansei Medical Co. Ltd., Kyoto, Japan) for 10 days at 37°C under 5% CO 2  in RPMI-1640 medium (Fujifilm Wako Pure Chemicals, Ltd., Osaka, Japan) containing 10% FBS (Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., Waltham, MA, USA), 40 ng/mL GM-CSF (BioLegend, San Diego, CA, USA), 20 ng/ml M-CSF (BioLegend, San Diego, CA, USA), and 50 μ/mL ampicillin (Sigma-Aldrich, St. Louis, MO, USA) (the RPMI-1640 medium). After 10 days, BMDMs were harvested for subsequent experiments.
