**Notes on grouping.** Rows 1 and 2 share one differentiation procedure and differ only in starting material (fresh vs. cryopreserved marrow) — that comparison is the paper's subject, so they are listed separately. Rows 7 and 8 are explicitly offered by the authors as alternatives to each other ("Alternative differentiation was carried out with 20 ng/mL recombinant murine M-CSF" **[6]**). PMC7047825 gives medium composition and duration but defers the mechanics to prior published procedures **[3]**; PMC11302253 likewise cites an earlier method and does not state an M-CSF concentration or source **[4]**.

Across the six papers there are effectively two families: **L929/L-cell conditioned medium** at 20–30% in DMEM or RPMI with 10–20% FBS (rows 1, 2, 4, 7), and **defined recombinant M-CSF** at 10–20 ng/mL (rows 3, 6, 8; row 5 unspecified). Differentiation is 5–10 days in every case, with a mid-course feed on day 4 (rows 1, 2, 6) or days 2 and 4 (rows 7, 8).
