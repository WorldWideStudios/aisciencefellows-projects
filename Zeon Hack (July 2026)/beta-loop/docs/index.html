workflow · mastermix/workflows/tem1_activity_screen.json

Running the combined TEM-1 screen

One workflow file now does the dilution and dosing job end to end: it dilutes its own reagents — TEM-1 enzyme, 9 active test compounds plus a known-active control, matched vehicle, and (just-in-time) nitrocefin — then builds a 36-well assay plate, mixes it, pre-incubates, and develops the color reaction in a stagger-aware order. It then stops: the plate reader is no longer touched by the robot at all.

01Five phases, then a hand-off

The shape of the protocol

Every node falls into one of five phases, executed strictly in sequence (every edge is on_success — no branching, no retries). The robot's job now ends at phase V; reading the plate is a separate, manual step.

0 Prepare working solutions 1 skill, 5 dilutions
I Build the plate 14 batched dispenses
II Whole-plate mix shaker, 1 min
III Pre-incubate bench wait, 10 min
IV Nitrocefin, just-in-time 1 prep + 13 dispenses
V Save run · robot stops plate reader is manual now
02Phase 0

Preparing every working solution before assay setup

The very first node, prepare_dilutions, runs the prepare_tem1_dilution_plate skill before the workflow even picks up a pipette for the assay itself. It performs five distinct dilutions on wellplate_pcr_parts_4 (the working plate) and in cold-block tubes:

1
TEM-1 enzyme, 2-step dilution 100 ng/µL → 0.1 ng/µL
2 µL stock (hole_1) + 198 µL BLB (hole_6) → 200 µL at 1 ng/µL intermediate (hole_2), mixed; then 100 µL of that + 900 µL more BLB → 1000 µL at 0.1 ng/µL working solution (hole_8), mixed again. A real 100× then 10× dilution — 1000× total.
2
T1262 tazobactam (now Compound 1), special low-dose intermediate 10 mM → 200 µM
49 µL BLB (hole_5) + 1 µL of 10 mM T1262 stock — read from compound_1_source_plate/compound_1_source_well — → 50 µL at 200 µM in hole_3, mixed. Every other compound targets a 50 µM assay dose (per literature, most default to a project-wide HTS convention); T1262's is set from a 10× IC50 rule, landing at 1 µM, so it gets its own intermediate tube instead of the standard recipe.
3
Batch-dispense BLB into 10 working wells batched · 47.5 µL
Positive control + all 9 active compound wells (including T1262's) get their diluent from BLB tube 1 (hole_5) in one multi-dispense pass. Slot 10 no longer exists in the workflow's compound list at all — as of "fix compound 10," it was removed outright rather than left empty.
4
Batch-dispense BLB into the vehicle well batched · 95 µL
From BLB tube 2 (hole_6) — a second physical tube exists purely to keep either tube under a ~1.5 mL practical fill limit, not for chemistry reasons.
5
Transfer stock/intermediate + DMSO into every well, then mix all 11 10× stock + 1× DMSO
9 compounds + positive control each get 2.5 µL from their own source well — except Compound 1 (T1262), which gets 2.5 µL from its 200 µM intermediate (hole_3) instead of raw stock. Vehicle gets 5 µL DMSO. All 11 wells are then mixed (5 cycles).

Result: positive control + 8 standard compounds at 500 µM, T1262 at 10 µM, vehicle at 5% DMSO, and TEM-1 at 0.1 ng/µL — all sitting ready before Phase I even starts.

04Phase II & III

Mix, then wait for compounds to meet TEM-1

Unchanged from prior versions. The plate takes an out-and-back trip to the shaker (13 discrete open/load/close/run/wait/unload/return skill calls, since the shaker can't be trusted to hold or release the plate on its own), then sits through a 10-minute room-temperature pre-incubation before anything reacts with nitrocefin.

place pipette→ grab plate→ open · load · close shaker→ run · on→ wait 1 min→ run · off→ open · unload · close shaker→ return plate · grab pipette→ pre-incubate 10 min
05Phase IV

Nitrocefin, prepared just-in-time and dosed in a staggered order

New skill: prepare_nitrocefin_working_solution runs right after pre-incubation ends — not back in Phase 0 — because the working solution is meant to go onto the plate immediately after it's made.

1
Dilute nitrocefin, 20 mM → 100 µM just-in-time
1243.75 µL BLB (hole_7, chunked across pipette-volume-limited transfers) + 6.25 µL of 20 mM stock (hole_4) → 1250 µL at 100 µM in hole_10, then mixed. This is the "2×" nitrocefin: still 2-fold above its 50 µM in-well target, because 25 µL lands in a 50 µL well.
2
Dose 13 conditions in stagger-aware order 13× batched · 25 µL
Order: no-TEM-1 negative → positive control → compounds 1–9 → vehicle + TEM-1 last. The vehicle wells have no inhibitor at all, so they're the fastest-reacting condition on the plate — dosing them last minimizes the uncontrolled reaction time they accumulate before the first read.

08Phase V

The robot stops; the reader is manual now

Place pipette → save_run_folder → end. That's it — no grab_plate, no open_reader/load_reader/close_reader, no run_kinetic_schedule, no platereader input at all. The canvas now says it directly: "The robot stops after nitrocefin addition and run-log capture. Move the plate to the reader manually and start the A490 method from Gen5."

This replaces the previous version's fully-automated kinetic read (21 scripted single-reads via platereader_run_kinetic_schedule). That approach had a real problem: each single-read click sequence in platereader_measure takes ~100+ seconds of GUI automation, far longer than the intended 30 s interval between reads — so the recorded read timestamps didn't match when the reads actually happened. Handing this to a human running Gen5's own native kinetic protocol sidesteps that entirely: Gen5 handles the internal 30 s timing itself.

What still exists, just relocated

  • platereader_run_kinetic_schedule and the read_schedule.py timing math are unchanged — they're just no longer wired into this production workflow
  • A new isolated workflow, test_platereader_kinetic_schedule.json, exercises the skill directly with a 3-read, 2-second schedule — fast enough to smoke-test in simulation without pretending it represents a real hardware timing guarantee
  • kinetic_second_read_s, reader_temperature_c, slope_window_start_s, etc. are gone from tem1_activity_screen.json's inputs — that configuration now lives on the Gen5 side, set by the operator

Every nitrocefin dispense node (Phase IV) still carries a timing_label (positive_control, compound_1, …) — batched_dispense_mastermix records the actual wall-clock moment nitrocefin hits each condition. Since dosing is staggered across ~13 batches, not simultaneous, this timestamp is what lets analysis line up each condition's own reaction start against whatever clock the manually-run Gen5 kinetic read produces.
