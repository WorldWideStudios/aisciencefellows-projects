Phase 2 — Assay Loading
3
Dispense TEM-1 Working Solution dispense
Add 40 µL manually prepared TEM-1 working solution (hole_8) to all 27 positive-control and test-compound wells in batches.
27 wells 40 µL / well 0.04 ng/µL final
4
Dispense No-Enzyme Prep dispense
Add 40 µL BLB/no-enzyme prep (hole_6) to the 3 negative-control wells: D3, D7, E11.
3 wells 40 µL / well No TEM-1
5
Dispense Vehicle dispense
Add 10 µL matched vehicle (working plate A2) to no-enzyme control wells D3, D7, E11.
3 wells 10 µL / well 0.75% total DMSO
6
Dispense Positive Control dispense
Add 10 µL positive-control working solution (T1262 Tazobactam, A1) to wells F3, F7, G11.
3 wells 10 µL / well 50 µM final ≥50% inhibition expected
7
Dispense Test Compounds 1–8 dispense
Add 10 µL per compound working solution to its three replicate wells. Each compound is dispensed in sequence from the working plate.
C1 T1262 → B2, D2, F2 C2 T6685 → B4, D4, F4 C3 T14081 → B6, D6, F6 C4 T1008 → B10, D10, F10
C5 T0224 → C5, E5, G5 C6 T0985 → C9, E9, G9 C7 T0138 → C3, E3, G3 C8 T8390 → C7, E7, G7
Phase 3 — Seal & Shake
8
Place Pipette Back mechanical
Return current pipette to stand before sealing operations begin.
9
Seal Plate mechanical
Seal the assay plate on PlateMax, return to home fixture (wellplate_holder_tags). Peels seal index 1 from stacked holder.
PlateMax sealer Grasp short-side Return to home
10
Load Sealed Plate into Shaker mechanical
Ensure shaker off → open lid → grab sealed plate → load into slot_1 → close lid.
Ensure shaker off → Open lid → Grab short-side → Load slot_1 → Close lid
11
Shaker Mix wait · 1 min
Run shaker for 1 minute for whole-plate compound mixing.
12
Unload & Return Sealed Plate mechanical
Stop shaker → open lid → unload plate → close lid → return sealed plate to home fixture.
Stop shaker → Open lid → Unload plate → Close lid → Return to home
Phase 4 — Sealed Incubation
13
Sealed Pre-Incubation wait · 10 min
Incubate the sealed assay plate at room temperature for 10 minutes. Workflow halts here. Operator manually unseals and adds nitrocefin outside the workflow.
Phase 5 — Teardown
14
Save Run Folder teardown
Bundle run log and timing artifacts into tem1_activity_screen_manual_nitro_r3_v1/. Protocol ends — proceed to manual nitrocefin addition.
