# How to remove a sealing mat

This procedure outlines the correct steps for removing a rubber sealing mat from a 1 mL deep-well plate, suitable for programming a robot.

## Procedure

1.  **Secure the plate by holding it from the sides and pressing it against the table.** [V1@00:15-00:37], [V2@00:06-00:11], [V3@00:06-00:28], [V4@00:05-00:13]
2.  **Grip an edge or corner of the rubber mat.** [V1@00:37-00:57], [V2@00:11-00:15], [V3@00:10-00:15], [V4@00:13-00:19]
3.  **Slowly and gently peel the mat diagonally across the plate, moving it upwards.** [V1@00:57-01:15], [V2@00:15-00:34], [V3@00:15-00:28], [V4@00:19-00:36]
4.  **Gently place the peeled mat on the table surface.** [V1@01:15-01:23], [V2@00:34-00:38], [V3@00:30-00:31], [V4@00:36-00:42]
