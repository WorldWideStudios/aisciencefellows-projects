# What not to do

This document describes common errors and their consequences when removing a rubber sealing mat from a 1 mL deep-well plate.

## Failure Mode: Contamination due to overly fast and forceful mat removal

*   **What was done wrong:** The rubber mat was peeled off too quickly and forcefully from the deep-well plate [V5].
*   **Visible symptom:** Blue liquid from the wells splashed onto the surface of the plate and the removed rubber mat, indicating contamination [V5@00:16].
*   **Camera detection:** This symptom is detectable by a camera [V5].
*   **Verbatim quote:** "Now we're gonna show improper way to peeling the rubber mat of the one mil dish well played. We hold it tightly, we hold the mat on the side and then we move faster. Whoa, look at that drop with. We see how the elements of our protein extract are contaminated and they are outside of the plate. This is failed experiment." [V5]

## Failure Mode: Cross-contamination during mat removal

*   **What was done wrong:** The rubber mat was peeled off too quickly and forcefully from the well plate [V6].
*   **Visible symptom:** Blue liquid splashed onto the table, and blue liquid is visible on the underside of the rubber mat and on the top surface of the well plate, indicating liquid transfer between wells [V6@00:13].
*   **Camera detection:** This symptom is detectable by a camera [V6].
*   **Verbatim quote:** "We're going to do improper peeling of the rubber mat from the one meal dip dish well plate. Hold it tightly on the side, but not too tight, hold mat on the side and pull it. Oh. And then we contaminated it on the lid and then liquid or protein extract is moving between the wells. This is complete failure." [V6]

## Failure Mode: Mat failing to detach due to insufficient upward pressure

*   **What was done wrong:** The operator attempted to peel the mat from the deep-well plate without applying sufficient upward pressure [V7].
*   **Visible symptom:** The rubber mat remains sealed to the deep-well plate, failing to detach when pulled. The blue liquid in the wells remains contained [V7@00:15].
*   **Camera detection:** This symptom is detectable by a camera [V7].
*   **Verbatim quote:** "it doesn't pull because we don't apply enough upward pressure. The mat stays sealed. Task is incomplete." [V7]

## Failure Mode: Plate flying out and spilling contents

*   **What was done wrong:** The rubber mat was pulled from one corner and moved upward too quickly, causing the deep-well plate to fly out and spill its contents [V8].
*   **Visible symptom:** Blue liquid is spilled and cross-contaminated on the rubber mat and around the wells of the deep-well plate. The plate also flips over [V8@00:17].
*   **Camera detection:** This symptom is detectable by a camera [V8].
*   **Verbatim quote:** "We're going to show improper way of peeling the rubber mat from the 1 milliliter deep dish well plate. Hold it from the side, not too tightly. Pull it pull the mat from one corner, move upward and then the plate flies out and we see the elements of the protein extract cross contaminated around the lid around the mat. This is failure." [V8]

## Failure Mode: Cross-contamination from improper mat disposal and re-placement

*   **What was done wrong:** The rubber mat was placed face down on the table after being removed from the plate, and then picked up and placed back on the plate [V9].
*   **Visible symptom:** Blue liquid residue (cross-contamination) is visible on the table where the mat was placed [V9@00:26].
*   **Camera detection:** This symptom is detectable by a camera [V9].
*   **Verbatim quote:** "This is failure because we put it the same way how it was on the plate. And we have cross contamination that you can visibly see on the table from multiple wells. This is failure." [V9]
