What this demonstrates

Two existing toy-photograph model comparisons, recorded YOLOE and OWLv2 boxes, a recorded ORDER classifier on some OWLv2 proposals, exact-byte image/source relationships and a human-inspectable interface. No detector weights are served; no new inference runs in the browser.

What remains unknown

No expert-confirmed taxonomy or independent accuracy benchmark for these images, no calibrated live-webcam pipeline at this website, no evidence that a robot recovered any specimen, and no new signed/Merkle Mountain Range commitment. Ultralytics weights and OWLv2 have separate upstream terms.
