BaeBugs

Bags in, barcodes out, and every step hashed. A modular open-science virtual lab for insect sample prep.

biobitworks · github.com/biobitworks/baebugs

Open on the one-line pitch: a camera looks at a preserved sample before it is sieved, sizes every body in millimetres, plans the sieve stack, checks the plan against a digital twin of the bench, and hands each fraction to BugPicker. Everything it does is a hashed leaf in one graph, so anyone can check it or contest it.
