Step 1 of the BioKEA line: BugSorter

Look before you sieve, then prove what happened

1 Observe

YOLO and classical morphometrics size every body in mm; disagreements are flagged, not sieved blind.

2 Hypothesize

Size classes become a coarse-to-fine sieve stack, a flow per sieve and a water budget.

3 Preflight

A digital twin of the bench rejects impossible plans before any hardware moves.

4 Run

The arm places sieves and water jets rinse; printed filter trays and parts are in the repo.

5 Verify

Each fraction is imaged; predicted vs observed updates the retention margin.

6 Hand off

Fractions go to BugPicker in its own manifest format, with a hash-chained provenance record.

Six steps, each one logged as a hashed record. The point of step 3 is that nothing reaches the arm unless the twin says the plan is physically valid: the sieve exists and is free, the order is right, and the water stays above reserve. Step 6 plugs straight into BugPicker with no new protocol.
